# Evidence Review: Dietary, Nutritional, and Lifestyle Interventions in Infertility Care

Welcome to this repository. This project provides a comprehensive and objective review of current clinical evidence—focusing primarily on Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs) and meta-analyses—regarding dietary, nutritional, supplemental, and lifestyle interventions in both female and male infertility.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- Reproductive endocrinologists, urologists, pharmacists, embryologists, and other healthcare professionals.
- Dietary supplement manufacturers and professionals in the fertility and wellness industry.
- Researchers seeking an evidence-based summary of nutritional interventions in reproductive medicine.

## 💡 Key Takeaway
A central theme across this review is the critical distinction between **intermediate biological outcomes** (e.g., improved sperm DNA fragmentation, semen parameters, or oocyte yield) and **patient-important clinical outcomes** (i.e., live birth). 
This report highlights which interventions show promising intermediate signals (such as CoQ10) and which ones have had their clinical interpretation changed as higher-quality evidence has emerged (such as DHEA), based on the latest data available as of September 2026.
